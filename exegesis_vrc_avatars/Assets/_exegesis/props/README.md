# Screwdriver
Use to remove and reinstall panels, as well as some other capabilities. The screwdriver has a contact sender tagged `screwdriver`.

The evil screwdriver features SPS.

## Requirements
- [VRChat Avatars SDK 3.10.6](https://creators.vrchat.com/sdk/) or newer
- [Poiyomi 11.0.27](https://www.poiyomi.com/download/#method-2-alcom--creator-companion) or newer
- [VRCFury 1.1437](https://vrcfury.com/download/) or newer
- [TheCodeWarrior](https://github.com/thecodewarrior)'s [PS1 module for Poiyomi](https://discord.com/channels/550477546958094348/553319525387599910/1555713076618334228) ([thecodewarrior.dev](https://thecodewarrior.dev/); **included in this package** at `Assets/Editor/Poi_PS1/`, redistributed with permission)

## Installation
1. Download the package and drag into your unity *Project* tab
2. Enable the PS1 module (`Assets/Editor/Poi_PS1/`) in Poiyomi's module settings, so Poiyomi rebuilds its shaders with it
3. From the *Project* tab, Drag your screwdriver of choice into the *Scene* tab
4. In the *Scene* tab, move the screwdriver into the palm of your avatar's right hand
5. In the *Heirarchy* tab, drag the screwdriver into your avatar

## Usage
The screwdriver creates a sub-menu in your expressions (radial) menu called `Tools/`. Once enabled, you can make the `fist` gesture with your right hand to enable the `screwdriver` contact sender, which removes panels on Exegesis avatars. (The contact sender becomes disabled if the SPS plug is enabled.)
